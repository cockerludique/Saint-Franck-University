PERSONAL JOURNAL

27 JULY 1996

Everyone says something different.

Thomas saw her leave.
Ryan says she wasn't alone.
Gregory says she left alone.

I don't know who is lying.

But someone is.

29 JULY 1996

Jessica remembers her talking to someone.
Thomas saw her leave.
Ryan saw her leave with someone.

Three different stories.

None of them fit.

31 JULY 1996

I found an older version of the article.

Someone changed it.

Not deleted.
Changed.

Why?

04 AUGUST 1996

She told me about someone she liked.

She said "she."

I think I know who she meant.

I don't know if I'm ready to write it down.

05 AUGUST 1996

Someone told me to leave it alone.

I didn't.

10 AUGUST 1996

I found the reset schedule.

August 15.

12 AUGUST 1996

If something happens to me, look at the old versions.

Don't trust the current pages.

14 AUGUST 1996 — 23:47

Tomorrow they reset everything.

They think that means the old information is gone.

It isn't.

I checked.

The old versions are still there.

I've left things where they won't look.

I don't know if anyone will ever find them.

If you are reading this:

Don't look for what they left.

Look for what they changed.
