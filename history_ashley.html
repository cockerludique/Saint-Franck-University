ASHLEY MORGAN
PRIVATE NOTES

----------------------------------------

23 JULY 1996

The campus is almost empty.

Most people have already left for
the summer.

The newspaper office is still open.

I should probably be working on the
next issue.

Instead I keep thinking about Kristin.

She says she wants to change
departments.

Journalism.

I think she would be good at it.

----------------------------------------

END OF ENTRY

ASHLEY MORGAN
PRIVATE NOTES

----------------------------------------

24 JULY 1996
17:36

Kristin came to see me again.

She asked whether I thought the houses
really mattered for someone's future.

I told her they shouldn't.

She said Professor Holloway had told
her otherwise.

----------------------------------------

18:04

She asked me something strange.

She asked if I thought people could
tell when you cared about someone.

I changed the subject.

----------------------------------------

18:47

I saw Gregory and Vicky talking.

They stopped when I walked past.

Morgan was with them earlier.

Something is wrong.

----------------------------------------

END OF ENTRY

VERSION 1.2

Created: 25 July 1996

Modified by: AM

ASHLEY MORGAN
PRIVATE JOURNAL

----------------------------------------

24 JULY 1996
19:12

Kristin asked me to leave with her
after the party.

I told her I would meet her later.

I should have gone.

----------------------------------------

20:03

She was talking about the houses again.

She said she had been told that joining
one of them would make everything easier.

I asked her who told her that.

She wouldn't say.

But I already knew.

----------------------------------------

21:05

I saw her near the North Campus.

She was upset.

She told me:

"They want me to prove myself."

I asked her what she meant.

She wouldn't explain.

----------------------------------------

21:31

I saw Gregory.

He told me I should stop asking
questions.

I asked him where Kristin was.

He smiled.

He said:

"She's learning."

----------------------------------------

21:52

I don't know where she is.

----------------------------------------

23:18

Everyone is pretending nothing happened.

I don't believe them.

----------------------------------------

END OF ENTRY

VERSION 1.3

Created: 26 July 1996

Author: AM

Status: HIDDEN 


ASHLEY MORGAN
PRIVATE JOURNAL

----------------------------------------

25 JULY 1996

I don't know what happened to Kristin.

Everyone says I should stop asking.

Gregory won't answer me.

Vicky won't look at me.

Morgan looks like he hasn't slept.

And Professor Holloway told me that
some questions are better left unanswered.

I don't believe him.

----------------------------------------

LATER

I remember three things.

The place where they said she would be.

The person who told them to do it.

And the thing Vicky changed afterwards.

I won't write their names.

If someone finds this, they'll know
where to look.

----------------------------------------

One more thing.

Kristin wasn't afraid of the houses.

She was afraid of what they would do
if they discovered who she really cared
about.

----------------------------------------

I think they already knew.

----------------------------------------

END OF ENTRY
