PRIVATE JOURNAL

Recovered from the 1995-1996 archive.

23 JULY 1996


The newspaper office is almost empty.

Everyone is talking about going home
for the summer.

Kristin stayed.

She says she wants to change
departments.

I told her she should do it.

She is better at this than she thinks.

She asked me whether the houses
really mattered.

I said no.

She didn't seem convinced.

----------------------------------------

AM

24 JULY 1996


17:36

Kristin came to see me.

She asked if I thought people could
tell when you cared about someone.

I asked what she meant.

She changed the subject.

----------------------------------------

18:04

She talked about Professor Holloway.

She said he thought joining one of
the houses would help her future.

She asked me if I knew which one.

I told her she didn't need them.

----------------------------------------

18:47

I saw Gregory and Vicky talking.

They stopped when I walked past.

Morgan was there earlier.

I don't like this.

----------------------------------------

19:22

Kristin left.

She said she would see me later.

----------------------------------------

END OF ENTRY

24 JULY 1996 — LATER


20:57

I saw Kristin near the North Campus.

She looked upset.

I asked what happened.

She said:

"They want me to prove myself."

I asked who.

She wouldn't answer.

----------------------------------------

21:05

She said she had to go.

She told me:

"Don't come looking for me."

I asked why.

She smiled.

It didn't look like her usual smile.

----------------------------------------

21:31

I saw Gregory.

I asked where Kristin was.

He said:

"She's learning."

I asked what that meant.

He told me to stay out of it.

----------------------------------------

21:52

I can't find her.

----------------------------------------

22:17

Vicky came back to the student center.

She was carrying something.

I couldn't see what it was.

She wouldn't talk to me.

----------------------------------------

END OF ENTRY

25 JULY 1996


Everyone says Kristin will turn up.

Nobody seems worried.

I am.

----------------------------------------

I asked Morgan what happened.

He told me:

"Forget it."

----------------------------------------

I asked Gregory.

He laughed.

----------------------------------------

I asked Vicky.

She wouldn't look at me.

----------------------------------------

Professor Holloway came to the
newspaper office.

He asked what I knew.

I told him nothing.

He said:

"That's probably for the best."

----------------------------------------

I don't believe him.

----------------------------------------

END OF ENTRY

26 JULY 1996


I know something happened.

I just don't know exactly what.

There are three people who know.

Maybe four.

----------------------------------------

I won't write their names here.

If someone finds this file,
they will know where to look.

----------------------------------------

There is one thing I am certain of:

Kristin trusted the wrong people.

----------------------------------------

And I think Professor Holloway
knows much more than he says.

----------------------------------------

END OF ENTRY
